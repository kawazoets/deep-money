FY ended December 2025 is **nine months** following a change in fiscal year end. Never compare it directly to 12-month years without adjusting duration and seasonality. “Operating CF less software and PP&E cash expenditure” is an analyst-defined indicator, **not** reported or normalized free cash flow.

FY ended December 2025 operating cash flow was helped by increases in accounts payable/accruals and deferred revenue. The company did not disclose an FY2026 earnings forecast because it could not reasonably estimate one; this predates the October 2026 data incident.

The 2025-03 financing cash flow was **+5,778.374**, materially supported by **+5,723.899** from noncontrolling shareholders. That cash inflow was external capital, not evidence of sustainable operating cash generation.

FY ended December 2025 financing CF was **-645.066**, including -500 short-term borrowing change, +300 new long-term loans and -445.734 long-term debt repayments. This does not prove future loan rollover terms.

## Conditional liquidity sensitivity, not a forecast

Starting consolidated cash equivalents 2026-06-30: JPY 4,408.787m. Baseline interest-bearing debt 4,384.132m (see Phase 2). **Do not net debt and cash when calculating runway; debt refinancing and repayments have different timing.**

Cash usage after selected capital expenditures, at 2026 H1 pace, would be approximately **JPY 80.255m/month**. This is a *mechanical* 6-month average and not a reliable forward cash-burn estimate.

At that purely illustrative burn rate, without refinancing or new capital:
- JPY 500m one-time incident-related cash outflow corresponds to ~6.2 months of this modeled expenditure.
- JPY 1,000m corresponds to ~12.5 months.
- JPY 2,000m corresponds to ~24.9 months.

These are **scenario input amounts, not estimated or announced incident costs**. They are not an actual solvency horizon; cash restrictions, debt repayment, seasonality, working capital, growth spending and subsidiary ownership must be modeled before computing one.

## Critical acquisition-relevant findings

1. FY2025-March financing relied heavily on minority shareholder contributions. Parent shareholders do not automatically own all subsidiary value.
2. Standalone operating recovery and group cash funding are separate questions. Identify who holds cash and who owes debt.
3. A buyer may seek particular software assets rather than assume all group liabilities. Asset-sale proceeds and existing shareholders' residual value must be modeled separately.
4. Rosetta Stone rights transfer to IXL Learning was disclosed in February 2026. Historical revenues and asset carrying values may not reflect current perimeter.
5. **No supportable stock-price floor or takeover value is computed yet.**
