# Phase 4 — POCKETALK funding valuation and takeover sensitivities
Research date: 2026-10-17. Currency: JPY million unless otherwise indicated.

## Material new primary disclosures

**2026-06-30:** POCKETALK Corporation raised third-party equity from Agenda Co., Ltd. SOURCENEXT disclosed a **post-money valuation of JPY 31,240 million** (JPY 312.4 oku) and its own **post-issue ownership of 68.82%**. This is a transaction-implied company valuation, NOT a cash offer for SOURCENEXT or a confirmed market price for SOURCENEXT's stake.

**2026-10-13:** POCKETALK disclosed its first consolidated **monthly operating profit** in September 2026. US subsidiary September operating margin 31.8%; Europe subsidiary 26.7%. Q3 year-over-year operating-profit improvement at US + Europe combined: JPY 207m. These are single-month and subsidiary figures; do not extrapolate to annual consolidated profits without revenue and seasonality.

## Equity valuation bridge: arithmetic, not a price target

| Item | Amount |
|---|---:|
| POCKETALK post-money implied equity valuation | 31,240.000 |
| SOURCENEXT ownership | 68.82% |
| Pro-rata implied value of SOURCENEXT stake | 21,497.368 |
| SOURCENEXT shares outstanding excluding treasury (2026-06-30) | 138,314,976 shares |
| Illustrative SOURCENEXT share price (2026-10-16 close) | JPY 105 |
| Implied listed market capitalization at that price | JPY 14,523.072m |
| Pro-rata POCKETALK stake value per SOURCENEXT share | ~JPY 155.42 |

**Do not interpret the ~JPY 155.42 as fair value or a floor.** It ignores POCKETALK's minority-investor rights, preferred rights if any, liquidity discount, transfer restrictions, debt allocation, tax, holding-company expenses, parent liabilities, other business value, and differences between the financing's price-setting and an arm's-length acquisition. It also assumes the disclosed 68.82% still applies after any subsequent financing.

## Transaction-implied stake valuation haircut sensitivity

These are **illustrative discounts** applied to the disclosed financing-implied pro-rata stake value, not estimates of realizable value or future stock prices.

| Hypothetical discount to pro-rata POCKETALK stake value | Residual stake value JPY million | Per SOURCENEXT share JPY |
|---|---:|---:|
| 0% | 21,497.368 | 155.42 |
| 25% | 16,123.026 | 116.57 |
| 50% | 10,748.684 | 77.71 |
| 75% | 5,374.342 | 38.86 |
| 90% | 2,149.737 | 15.54 |

**These values are not takeover consideration.** An acquisition or rescue valuation requires adding other assets and business value and subtracting liabilities, transaction costs, incident costs, minority claims, taxes, and dilution. The stake cannot necessarily be monetized at any haircut.

## Why the 2026-10 incident does not mechanically destroy all group value

The publicly described breach involves SOURCENEXT customer information. Its direct economic impact on POCKETALK's independent business, licensing arrangements and brand is **not established**. Distinguish the incident's impact on SOURCENEXT standalone operations from contagion to subsidiaries. Conversely, an acquirer may discount a group for reputational, financing or control reasons.

## Crucial open questions

1. Obtain the 2026-06-30 funding notice's detailed security terms and any special investor preferences (not included in short release).
2. Confirm whether 68.82% is fully diluted and current; identify minority investor protections and any transfer limitations.
3. Confirm whether 31,240m is a negotiated post-money value for common shares or a headline valuation with non-standard terms.
4. Allocate cash, debt, investment securities and cash burn between parent and POCKETALK, avoiding double counting in sum-of-parts.
5. Obtain comparable transaction values for translation SaaS, language devices and Japanese software distribution, separating recurring from one-off sales.
6. Track monthly profit recovery without equating one positive month to sustained profitability.

## Research standard

Distinguish financing-implied valuation, public market capitalization, net asset value, asset-sale value, and actual acquisition consideration. Never describe a funding valuation as a takeover bid.
