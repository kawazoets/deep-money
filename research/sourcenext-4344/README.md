# Sourcenext (4344) — Incident, Solvency and Downside Valuation

**Research status:** Phase 1 — source verification and baseline, 17 October 2026.  
**Disclosure:** The researcher has purchased shares in Sourcenext. Position size and purchase price are not disclosed. This research is not investment advice.

## Questions to answer numerically

1. How much readily available liquidity remains, after debt maturities, restricted cash, operating cash burn and other obligations?
2. How much incremental cost and lost business could the October 2026 data incident create?
3. What are the addressable markets, competitive prospects and realistic standalone values of the company's business segments?
4. Under standalone recovery, distressed financing, asset sale and takeover scenarios, what equity value **per share** remains for existing shareholders?
5. At which share prices would Prime Market continued-listing requirements become an issue, taking actual free-float share count into account?

**No conclusion such as “further investigation is necessary” is sufficient.** Each material unknown must be accompanied by its valuation sensitivity, where estimable.

## Model specification (not yet populated)

**Liquidity runway** = (unrestricted cash + realistically drawable committed funding − mandatory near-term payments − minimum operating cash reserve) / normalized monthly **net cash burn**. This is a scenario metric, not simply net assets divided by accounting losses.

**Equity value** = enterprise value of operating businesses + excess cash + non-operating realizable assets − debt − debt-like liabilities − transaction/restructuring costs − incident liabilities, adjusted for minority interests and any new equity issuance.

**Per-share value** = residual equity value attributable to current shareholders / fully diluted shares. Model takeover consideration, asset purchase, debt restructuring and liquidation separately; a buyer's enterprise price is not necessarily the price received by current shareholders.

**Incident sensitivity**: construct independently supported ranges for one-off remediation, multi-year churn, potential legal/regulatory exposure, incremental cybersecurity spend, and funding dilution. Avoid assuming a fixed cost per record without comparables.
