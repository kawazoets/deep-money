## New investment interpretation
POCKETALK valuation at 31.24bn is forward-expectations heavy against shrinking 2026 H1 revenue and persistent losses, even as September 2026 reported single-month operating break-even. This is materially stronger evidence than product-category sales and arbitrary discounting.
