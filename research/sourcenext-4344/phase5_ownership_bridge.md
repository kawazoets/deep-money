# Phase 5 — ownership bridge and the limits of takeover valuation
As of 2026-10-17.

## Critical distinction

The JPY 31.24bn post-money valuation of POCKETALK announced on 2026-06-30 is a **financing-implied equity valuation**, not an acquisition offer. SOURCENEXT's 68.82% pro-rata exposure implies JPY 21.497bn **before** discounts and minority investor rights. This number cannot simply be added to consolidated book equity: doing so would double count POCKETALK's assets and liabilities already consolidated.

The correct bridge is:
- Mark the parent's economic ownership of POCKETALK on a fully diluted, security-rights-adjusted basis.
- Separately value the parent-only operating businesses and investments **excluding** POCKETALK.
- Deduct parent-only net debt and other claims, and incorporate contingent incident liabilities, tax leakage, transaction costs, and rescue financing dilution.
- Divide the equity attributable to existing SOURCENEXT shareholders by the relevant fully diluted shares.

The 2026-06-30 consolidated net assets of JPY 5.836bn include noncontrolling interests and share acquisition rights; do not treat all consolidated net assets as attributable to SOURCENEXT common shareholders. The June balance sheet is pre-incident.

## What can be computed now

- 2026-06-30 common shares excluding treasury: 138,314,976.
- POCKETALK headline post-money valuation: JPY 31,240m.
- Pro-rata 68.82% stake: JPY 21,497.368m.
- Pro-rata stake headline per SOURCENEXT share: ~JPY 155.42, **not** a price floor.
- Every JPY 1,000m of **incremental** equity-value impairment (holding shares fixed) translates to ~JPY 7.23 per share. This is a sensitivity, not a prediction.
- Every JPY 1,000m of new capital issued at JPY 100/share creates 10m new shares before fees, warrants and other terms; this is hypothetical and not a disclosed financing plan.

## Unresolved items that block a credible downside target

1. Separate legal-entity balance sheets for parent and POCKETALK; intercompany loans, guarantees and collateral.
2. POCKETALK capital table, instrument seniority, anti-dilution and liquidation preferences.
3. Parent's stake carrying value and any impairments, so POCKETALK is not counted twice.
4. Debt refinancing calendar and secured versus unsecured claims.
5. Incident cost, customer churn and insurance coverage; none should be invented.
6. Standalone market and earnings forecasts for each business and comparable transaction multiples.

## Explicitly avoid these errors

- Equating consolidated gross cash with parent cash available to repay parent debt.
- Treating an unlisted subsidiary's financing headline as realizable sale proceeds.
- Treating an acquisition of the company, acquisition of assets, rescue funding and insolvency as interchangeable.
- Claiming JPY 155, JPY 34 or any other single figure is a downside floor.
