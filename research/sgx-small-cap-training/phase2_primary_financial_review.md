# SGX phase 2 — primary-source financial review (2026-10-27)

This note evaluates three *screening candidates*, not recommendations. Quote inputs from phase 1 are reference prices, not executable bids/offers. Verify broker board lots, spread, actual fees and corporate actions before trading.

## Thai Beverage (Y92)
- Primary IR: https://thaibev.listedcompany.com/ir.html
- 2026 nine months to June: revenue THB254.04bn vs THB258.62bn a year ago (down ~1.8%); EBITDA THB48.29bn vs THB45.03bn (+7.2%). Sources: 2026 3Q business update and Reuters 2026-08-13.
- Official IR displayed S$0.440 last trade, 31.15m volume on 2026-10-26. This is a high-volume candidate, but the latest spread still needs verification.
- Risks: declining sales, exposure to Thailand consumer demand, FX translation, debt. Do not mistake EBITDA improvement for revenue growth.

## PNE Industries (BDA)
- FY2025 primary annual report: https://pne.com.sg/industries/annualreport.pdf
- FY2025 revenue S$71.1m (+16.6%), gross margin 14.5% vs 18.6%, pretax profit ~S$0.7m; cash S$17.7m, no bank borrowings as of Sep 2025.
- FY2026 H1 to Mar 2026: revenue ~S$31.3m (down ~11% YoY), net loss ~S$0.668m vs prior profit. Primary: https://links.sgx.com/FileOpen/PNE%20-FY26H1%20Results%20Announcement.ashx?App=Announcement&FileID=888418
- Strong historical cash does not eliminate weak operating profitability or thin trading liquidity. Confirm bid/ask.

## Koda (BJZ)
- Primary FY2026 SGX release: https://links.sgx.com/FileOpen/Koda%20FY2026%20Results%20Announcement.ashx?App=Announcement&FileID=902656
- FY2026 ended June 30 revenue US$63.855m vs US$52.730m (+21.1%); profit for year US$2.203m vs US$0.576m.
- But operating cash flow was **negative US$5.2m** due to receivables/inventory working-capital expansion; cash and cash equivalents US$10.4m. Borrowing increased to fund working capital.
- Accounting earnings improvement does not establish cash conversion. Verify spread, volume, order book.

## Preliminary view
- Y92: best observed volume among these, though modest revenue contraction and debt.
- BDA: balance-sheet cash buffer, but deteriorating H1 earnings.
- BJZ: stronger earnings growth, yet negative operating cash flow.
- None is cleared for trading. Next: latest broker bid/ask, average daily turnover, minimum tick, optimized board lots within S$100 including costs, then compare spread-inclusive round-trip break-even.
