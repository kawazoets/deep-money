# Phase 11 — Test parent operating-business value against earnings evidence
Research date: 2026-10-17. JPY million unless stated.

## Evidence and comparability

- Parent standalone operating profit for the **nine-month** period April–December 2025: +274.099m (2025 annual securities report). Monthly average +30.46m.
- Parent standalone H1 2026 operating loss: approximately -220m (2026 H1 management presentation). Monthly average -36.67m.
- Difference between monthly averages: ~-67.12m/month. **Not a like-for-like YoY growth rate**: fiscal-year transition, seasonality and portfolio changes prevent a clean comparison.
- The February 12, 2026 Rosetta Stone Japan rights/business disposal decision changes the business perimeter. Sale price undisclosed. Historic parent profit is not normalized ongoing profit.
- The company disclosed FY2025 impairment of POCKETALK software JPY 1,209m and Rosetta Stone-related impairment JPY 408m consolidated / JPY 460m standalone; company describes impairments as noncash. These cannot be treated as recurring operating cash outflow. Conversely, future development expenditure and amortization may persist.

## Explicit EV / EBIT sensitivity

**These are deliberately hypothetical normalized annual EBITs, not forecasts.** Applying simple enterprise-value / EBIT multiples only to *positive* EBIT:

| Normalized parent annual EBIT (JPY m) | 5x EV/EBIT | 8x EV/EBIT | 12x EV/EBIT |
|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| 0 | 0 | 0 | 0 |
| 100 | 500 | 800 | 1,200 |
| 250 | 1,250 | 2,000 | 3,000 |
| 400 | 2,000 | 3,200 | 4,800 |

Multiples 5x/8x/12x are **scenario assumptions, not researched trading or M&A comparables**. EV/EBIT is inapplicable to persistently negative EBIT without additional assumptions; a distressed acquirer could assign positive strategic value to brands/customer base or negative value to unavoidable obligations.

FY2025 standalone nine-month EBIT +274.099m annualizes arithmetically to +365.465m, whereas H1 2026 -220m annualizes to -440m. Neither is a supported forecast. Their wide divergence demonstrates the sensitivity to profit normalization.

## Impact on Phase 10 scenario inputs

Phase 10 assumed parent EVs JPY 3,000m, 2,000m, 500m, and -500m. Under illustrative **8x EBIT**, these correspond to normalized annual EBIT +375m, +250m, +62.5m and not applicable for negative EV, respectively. The highest scenario requires a recovery close to the mechanical 2025 annualized profit; the 2026 H1 operating loss does not substantiate that recovery.

## Actionable conclusion

The valuation driver to research next is **whether the parent business can sustainably regain +250–375m annual operating profit after Rosetta Stone disposal and incident remediation**, not another iteration of checking for undisclosed standalone interim accounts. The key test is quarterly parent operating profitability and product/channel gross margin, plus cost reduction and lost-customer assumptions.

## Explicit limits

Do not mistake enterprise value for shareholder equity, or add the parent business EV to consolidated book equity. Parent-only debt and nonoperating assets must be bridged separately. No 2026 October price target is asserted.
