## What can and cannot be inferred

- **Cannot** infer full-year earnings from September margins: US/Europe monthly revenue denominators and group operating profit amount are not disclosed in the release.
- **Cannot** infer a market-supported EV/EBITDA or EV/revenue multiple from the financing valuation alone. Need independent annual revenue and profitability for the actual POCKETALK corporate perimeter, as well as capital structure and investment rights.
- 2024 monthly profitability followed by losses shows that the 2026 monthly profit announcement must be tested for persistence.
- Q3 operating profit improvement of JPY 207m is an **improvement versus previous-year quarter**, not the actual operating profit amount.
- **Corrected arithmetic:** 31,240m × 68.82% = JPY 21,499.368m pro-rata stake headline valuation, before discounts and claims.
