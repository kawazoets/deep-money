# Phase 12 — Cyber incident customer attrition and parent operating value sensitivity

Date: 2026-10-17. This is a **conditional sensitivity**, not an estimate of incident cost, customer loss, or stock price.

## Verified inputs and source

Parent standalone 2025 April–December (nine-month period):
- Revenue JPY 7093.965m.
- Gross profit JPY 3967.183m.
- Gross margin 55.92%.
- Operating profit JPY 274.099m.

See the 2025 annual securities report in company IR: https://www.sourcenext.co.jp/ir-english/library/annual/

The October 15 incident notice identifies up to 22,519,960 **potentially affected records**, but does not quantify lost revenue, attrition, remediation costs or customer compensation: https://www.sourcenext.com/support/i/2026/1015_info/

## Mechanistic sensitivity

Annualized historical parent revenue (simple 12/9 scaling): JPY 9458.620m. This ignores seasonality and portfolio changes including Rosetta Stone disposal. Assumed variable gross profit loss = sales loss × historic gross margin. SG&A is held fixed. Illustrative parent business EV/EBIT multiple = 8x, **not a verified peer multiple**.

At 1% persistent revenue decline, the model implies JPY 4.23 oku less parent operating enterprise value and JPY 3.06 per existing SOURCENEXT share **before debt, minority rights, taxes, dilution and any other claims**.

## Scenario interaction and non-double-counting

- One-time forensic investigation, notification, support and security remediation cash costs: separate variable.
- Permanent sales/customer attrition: affects recurring operating profit and its capitalized value, as above.
- Recurring additional security operating costs: separately reduce normalized EBIT; do not capitalize them again as one-time outflow.
- Temporary sales interruption: treat as discrete cash-flow loss, not necessarily a permanent 8x multiple loss.
- POCKETALK subsidiary valuation: do not automatically impose parent customer attrition on subsidiary, unless linked channels/contracts are evidenced.
- Debt refinancing, share issuance and deal transaction costs are separate adjustments.

## Limitations

This model uses 2025 standalone nine-month gross margin and a crude annualization, not post-incident 2026 revenue. Fixed SG&A, unchanged margin and the 8x multiple are assumptions. In a distress transaction, a profit multiple may not apply. Therefore no scenario probability or price target is assigned.

## What would falsify the downside thesis?

Verified retention and renewal rates, sales conversion, site traffic, software subscription revenue, stable parent operating margin, and disclosed remediation expenses in 2026 Q3/Q4 reporting. Conversely, declining renewals or a sustained increase in security operating costs would worsen normalized value.
