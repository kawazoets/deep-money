# Deep Money

**Follow the money beneath the headlines.**

Deep Money is an independent research project that investigates how capital actually moves beneath economic narratives, market headlines, and financial events.

Rather than stopping at questions such as:

- Are regional banks in trouble?
- Is AI investment a bubble?
- Are real estate prices too high?
- Are interest rates becoming dangerous?

Deep Money goes one layer deeper.

The objective is to examine the balance sheets, cash flows, capital structures, ownership, debt, and financial incentives behind these questions.

## Research Approach

The starting point is often a simple observation, anomaly, or question.

From there, the research moves toward primary data and financial evidence.

The basic process is:

**Observation → Question → Data → Structure → Evidence → Interpretation**

The objective is not to predict markets.

It is to understand the financial structures underneath them.

## Current Research

### Japan Shinkin Banks — Interest Rate Risk

Japan's return to a world with interest rates is exposing balance-sheet structures that were built during decades of near-zero rates.

The first Deep Money research project examines Japanese shinkin banks (credit unions), focusing on the relationship between:

- Deposits
- Loans
- Securities holdings
- Japanese government bonds
- Unrealized gains and losses
- Capital

The initial question is simple:

> Is the stress appearing at individual shinkin banks an isolated problem, or evidence of a broader structural pattern?

The research begins with nationwide screening and then moves into the balance sheets of individual institutions.

## Research Principles

Deep Money separates three things:

**Fact** — what the data shows.

**Interpretation** — what the data may mean.

**Hypothesis** — what should be investigated next.

These should not be confused.

Research will be updated as new evidence becomes available.
