# FINAL SUPPLEMENT — SOURCENEXT 4344 / POCKETALK (17 October 2026)
**Status: closed with explicitly unresolved disclosure gaps.** This document supersedes the unsupported valuation confidence of the original FINAL_REPORT.md. Not an investment recommendation.

## Two questions and determinations

### 1. Is September 2026 POCKETALK operating profitability sustainable?

**Not established.** Company release dated October 13 states consolidated **September only** operating profit was positive. US September operating margin 31.8%, European 26.7%; both foreign subsidiaries positive in August and September. Combined US+Europe Q3 operating-profit **improvement** versus 2025 Q3 was JPY 207m, **not the quarter's actual profit**. No consolidated September revenue or profit amount disclosed. Source: https://prtimes.jp/main/html/rd/p/000000128.000108954.html

From SOURCENEXT's 2026 H1 POCKETALK consolidated figures (JPY m): revenue 1,485; gross profit 783; SG&A 1,370; operating loss approximately 586 (rounding differences). H1 gross margin 783/1485=52.73%.

**Constant-cost H1 break-even test:** at fixed SG&A 1,370m and gross margin 52.73%, six-month break-even revenue = 1,370/(783/1,485) = **2,598.3m**, ~75.0% above H1 actual revenue 1,485m. This is a historical cost-structure stress test, **not** a forward forecast: June restructuring and recurring Sentio services can change both SG&A and gross margin. Another equivalent path is cost reduction, but no reliable post-restructuring consolidated expense run-rate was disclosed. Do not extrapolate a September profitable month into annual EBIT.

### 3. Reverse valuation: what must be true for JPY 105 per SOURCENEXT share?

Historical comparison price 2026-10-16 JPY 105 (not live); outstanding shares 138,314,976; implied equity market value JPY **14,523.07248m**. Parent-only net borrowing JPY 1,052.977m **as of 2025-12-31, stale**.

## Final judgement and close-out

The key risk is not whether the headline financing valuation exists—it does—but whether a historically loss-making, shrinking-revenue POCKETALK business can earn into that valuation, and whether SOURCENEXT can realize the pro-rata stake value. The current primary disclosures **do not establish either**. The strongest decision-useful result is the reverse-valuation hurdle, not a fabricated point-price target.

**No more document-search loops.** Reopen only after (a) consolidated POCKETALK Q3 or FY results with revenue/EBIT, (b) legally disclosed 2026 security rights, (c) material parent debt changes, or (d) quantified incident liabilities. All unresolved inputs remain marked unresolved.
