# Phase 14 — POCKETALK valuation-to-sales cross-check
Date: 2026-10-17.

## Verified facts
- 2026-06-30 POCKETALK third-party financing headline post-money equity valuation: JPY 31,240m. Source: https://prtimes.jp/main/html/rd/p/000000113.000108954.html
- 2025-03-31 FY (12 months) SOURCENEXT consolidated **POCKETALK product-category** revenue: JPY 3,979.444m.
- 2025-12-31 FY (9 months) SOURCENEXT consolidated **POCKETALK product-category** revenue: JPY 2,701.497m.
- Source: https://financialfilings.com/filings/sourcenext-corporation/annual-report/2026/33017138/ ; original filing https://www2.jpx.co.jp/disc/43440/140120260212555384.pdf
- POCKETALK US and Europe August 2026 operating profit margins: 14.9% and 29.5%, respectively; Europe first profitable 2026 H1, with company-reported annual JPY 300m-scale operating profit improvement from restructuring. Source: https://prtimes.jp/main/html/rd/p/000000124.000108954.html and https://prtimes.jp/main/html/rd/p/000000115.000108954.html

## Mechanical equity-valuation-to-product-revenue ratios
- 31,240 / 3,979.444 = **7.850x** using FY ended March 2025 (12 months).
- 31,240 / (2,701.497 × 12/9) = **8.674x** using FY ended December 2025 annualized mechanically.
- This is **NOT** conventional EV/revenue: numerator is headline post-money equity value (not enterprise value); denominator is parent-group product-category revenue, not independently verified POCKETALK corporate consolidated revenue. The fiscal-period normalization ignores seasonality.
- 2025 product-category revenue decreased from 3,979m in 12 months to 2,701m in 9 months, but the different durations prevent direct growth-rate comparison.

## What the new evidence means
A high valuation-to-historical-product-revenue ratio implies substantial expectations of future growth, high recurring-revenue share or margin improvement. It does not prove the valuation is excessive. Monthly regional operating margins are insufficient to establish annual group EBITDA or justify a valuation multiple. The European annual operating-profit improvement of JPY 300m is an improvement, not an annual absolute profit amount.

## Remaining gap and decision rule
Obtain audited or otherwise reliable POCKETALK legal-group revenue, operating profit, net debt, preferred investor terms and 2026 run-rate. If these cannot be verified, label the JPY 31.24bn financing value **not independently validated**. Do not retrofit arbitrary discounts or produce an unsupported fair-value point estimate.

## Integrity correction
The proper pro-rata 68.82% of JPY 31,240m is **JPY 21,499.368m**, not JPY 21,497.368m as some earlier interim files stated. Final model uses the corrected amount.
