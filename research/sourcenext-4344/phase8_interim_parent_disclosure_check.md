## Arithmetic bridge — what is known versus unknown

The financing-implied POCKETALK pro-rata parent stake valuation is JPY 21,497.368m, based on a disclosed JPY 31,240m post-money company valuation and 68.82% ownership at the June 2026 transaction. This is not an observable sale value.

A defensible SOURCENEXT shareholder sum-of-parts valuation requires:
1. POCKETALK stake's realizable value, adjusted for security rights, illiquidity, dilution and tax;
2. parent standalone operating business enterprise value;
3. parent-only net debt at the valuation date;
4. other investments excluding double counting;
5. contingent claims, incident remediation, lost business, deal costs and new share issuance.

**No credible share-price floor or takeover offer value can be published yet.**
