POCKETALK category represents ~29.1% of consolidated revenue; the remaining categories total JPY 6,572.798m (~70.9%). **Non-POCKETALK product categories are not automatically parent-only revenue**, because product categories are consolidated and can include group cross-sales and other entities.

Company states its operating businesses are presented as a **single reportable segment**. Product categories and distribution channels are disclosed but category-level operating profits are not. Do not infer profitability from revenue alone.

## Why this matters to acquisition value

A strategic acquirer might value the company's customer distribution channel, consumer software brands and recurring software rights separately from POCKETALK. The incident's impact could be more direct on SOURCENEXT's customer database and online sales than on POCKETALK's independently operated overseas distribution, but the exact allocation is not established.

The 2025 annual report also records impairment of software and contract-related intangible assets. The valuation must distinguish book-value write-downs from ongoing cash expenditures.
