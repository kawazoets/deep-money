# Phase 9 — Original EDINET half-year report audit (2026-06-30)

Research date: 2026-10-17.

**Original filing**: https://ssl4.eir-parts.net/doc/4344/yuho_pdf/S100YULA/00.pdf (EDINET filing S100YULA, filed 2026-08-07, 25 pages).

## Primary-source findings

1. The company explicitly states on PDF p.2 that it prepares interim consolidated financial statements and therefore does not present parent-only key financial metrics. The filing is not a source for a June 2026 standalone balance sheet.
2. PDF p.5 (management cash-flow discussion) states H1 2026 financing cash flow of JPY -199.120m; net short-term borrowing reduction JPY 500m, long-term loan repayments JPY 148m, bond issuance proceeds JPY 250m and proceeds from third-party subsidiary share issuance JPY 149m. These components do not alone reconstruct the entire financing cash-flow statement.
3. Consolidated cash equivalents JPY 4,408.787m and operating cash flow JPY +3.691m, from PDF pp.2 and 5. Not parent-only cash.
4. PDF p.3: adjusted-comparable H1 2026 group operating loss JPY -805m vs H1 2025 JPY -2,014m. Online channel JPY 2,750m (+21.7%); overseas JPY 837m (-29.1%); retail JPY 508m (-29.5%). Comparisons are company-adjusted because 2025 had a nine-month transition fiscal year.
5. PDF p.6: shares issued 139,115,100 as of June 30. Disclosed option grants include 574,000 shares at exercise price JPY 131 and 364,800 shares at exercise price JPY 1; these grants' exercise periods start in 2028/2029. These are not equivalent to shares outstanding today and require proper dilution treatment.

## What remains unverified

- Parent-only June 2026 cash, loans, and subsidiary-investment carrying amount.
- Exact parent guarantee exposures for POCKETALK and any cross-defaults or pledged shares.
- Bank-level maturity/refinancing schedules and covenants.
- POCKETALK financing security rights, preferences and fully diluted cap table.

A search within the original filing did not surface standalone guarantee or related-party schedules. **Absence of an identified disclosure is not evidence that there are no guarantees or contingent liabilities.**

## Implication for acquisition modeling

A parent-only equity bridge cannot be finalized from this interim filing. Preserve the December 2025 standalone figures as historical observations; never relabel them as June 2026 data. A conservative model should vary refinancing, incident outflows and POCKETALK stake realization independently, with an explicit unresolved-debt and contingent-claims field.
