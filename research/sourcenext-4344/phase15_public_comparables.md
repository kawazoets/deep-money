# Phase 15: POCKETALK public comparable screen (2026-10-17)

## Primary question
Can the 2026-06-30 POCKETALK post-money equity value of JPY 31,240m be independently validated against observable listed translation-industry valuations?

## Data
- POCKETALK post-money equity headline JPY 31,240m: https://straightpress.jp/company_news/detail?pr=000000113.000108954
- SOURCENEXT reported POCKETALK **product-category**, NOT independently verified POCKETALK corporate revenue: FY ended 2025-03 (12 months) JPY 3,979.444m; FY ended 2025-12 (9 months) JPY 2,701.497m: https://financialfilings.com/filings/sourcenext-corporation/annual-report/2026/33017138/
- POCKETALK headline equity / historical category revenue = 7.850x for FY2025-03; 8.674x using mechanical annualization of 2025-12 nine-month period. These are **not conventional EV/revenue multiples**.
- Listed translation and language-services comparator RWS Holdings PLC: FY ended 2025-09-30 revenue GBP 690.1m; adjusted EBITDA GBP 100.8m; reported PBT loss GBP 99.7m: https://www.investegate.co.uk/announcement/rns/rws-holdings--rws/final-results/9289229
- London Stock Exchange company-page observed instrument market capitalization GBP 538.19m (retrieval observation, not guaranteed 2026-10-17 closing quote): https://www.londonstockexchange.com/stock/RWS/rws-holdings-plc/company-page
- RWS observed equity market capitalization / FY2025 sales = 538.19/690.1 = **0.780x**.
- POCKETALK category ratio / RWS market-cap-to-revenue ratio = ~10.1x; **not an apples-to-apples valuation spread**.

## Comparability screen
- RWS: listed and publishes financial statements; business is primarily language services, localization and enterprise language technology, not primarily portable translator hardware. Mature/declining 2025 revenue, so a lower multiple is unsurprising. Financial metrics differ in perimeter, period, financing and growth profile.
- Timekettle and Vasco Electronics: direct device competitors, but no independently verified public equity valuations plus matching audited revenue from this screen. Do **not** invent multiples or use private website estimates as transaction prices.
- TransPerfect: 2025 revenue USD 1.32bn reported, but private; without observed equity value no comparable valuation multiple. https://www.globenewswire.com/news-release/2026/03/05/3250281/0/en/transperfect-billed-revenues-increase-7-in-2025-to-1-32-billion.html

## Conclusion
A POCKETALK financing-value-to-historical-product-revenue ratio of 7.85x is markedly higher than the 0.78x observable RWS market-cap-to-sales reference, **but this does not prove overvaluation**. The comparator is structurally different and the numerator/denominator for POCKETALK are not verified to share the same consolidated reporting perimeter. No defensible independent POCKETALK fair-value range follows from these observations alone.

## Remaining decisive evidence
(1) POCKETALK legal-group consolidated sales and operating profit, (2) recurring SaaS vs device sales mix and customer retention, (3) net debt, (4) investment security class/liquidation preferences, (5) truly comparable listed hardware-plus-recurring-revenue transactions.

## Stop rule
Do not translate an incomparable public-company multiple into a stock-price target. Record as a warning signal and preserve the unknowns.
